# Try a Scrolling Story

A change of image can turn a paragraph in a new direction. Read these two views as a short story, then change a sentence and see how the words and images work together.

{% include scrollstory/source-demo.html scenes=page.story_scenes %}

## Make one choice, then read again

Change a heading or replace a paragraph with your own words. Swap the images to see what their order does to the story. Move the text to the other side and consider which part of the photograph it leaves visible.

The preview is a small window onto a full page. The same text file uses Xanthan's background and image-change instructions to make a story that fills the reader's screen. You can copy this passage into a story page, then replace the words and photographs with your own. [Start Your Own Story]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/scrollstories/start-your-story) walks through making a copy you can keep.

For longer examples, read [Forest]({{ site.baseurl }}/scrollstories/forest/) for images and prose in motion, or [Trail]({{ site.baseurl }}/scrollstories/trail/) for a geographic story map. When you need a particular setting, the [background switching guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/scrollstories/bg-switch) gives the full reference.
